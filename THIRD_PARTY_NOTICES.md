# Third-party assets — AYLA

## Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library

**Copyright © 2020 Microsoft.** The 3D human body meshes, facial materials, clothing and character animation clips are derived from the [Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), distributed under the **MIT License**.

The specific optimized GLB editions used by AYLA come from the open-source [Avatar Studio](https://github.com/memoriesrohit/avatar-studio) repository, commit `2fd9ec82fbe1613d40ae38aadcca3f410725f740`, distributed under the MIT License.

Models selected for this prototype:
- `models/priya_casual.glb`: Ayla gameplay avatar (current temporary base, subject to further character customization).
- `models/leo_smart.glb`: Winston gameplay avatar (current temporary base, subject to further character customization).

Assets are loaded over the public jsDelivr GitHub CDN at runtime. No paid provider is required; an internet connection is needed to load them. The existing procedural character geometry remains an offline fallback.
